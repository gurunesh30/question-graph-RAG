
Unit 1: Process Management
- Process Control Block (PCB): Contains process state, PID, program counter.
- CPU Scheduling: First-Come-First-Served (FCFS), Shortest Job First (SJF), and Round Robin algorithms.
Unit 2: Memory Management
- Virtual Memory: Uses Paging and Segmentation.
- Page Replacement Algorithms: FIFO, LRU (Least Recently Used), and Optimal Page Replacement.
